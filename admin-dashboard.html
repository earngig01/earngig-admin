Current Platform Rules

Active earning rules

These are the current member earning and withdrawal rules enforced by the platform. Referral bonuses and Create & Earn writer earnings are separate from the normal daily earning limit.

Starter

Daily normal earning ₦200
Minimum withdrawal ₦3,000

Standard

Daily normal earning ₦400
Minimum withdrawal ₦6,000

Premium

Daily normal earning ₦800
Minimum withdrawal ₦12,000

Elite

Daily normal earning ₦1,600
Minimum withdrawal ₦24,000
Withdrawal window: 15th and 28th of every month, from 12:00 AM to 11:59 PM Nigeria time (Africa/Lagos).

Daily earning limit: Applies to normal member activities such as Tasks, Watch & Earn and Read & Earn.

Exempt: Referral bonuses and Create & Earn writer earnings are not included in the daily normal earning cap.
